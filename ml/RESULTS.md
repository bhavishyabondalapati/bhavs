# Recommender results

> **Two separate things are reported here. Do not mix them up.**
> 1. **MovieLens benchmark** (public data, thousands of users and items): this is where model quality is measured.
> 2. **bhavs production run** (17 titles, mostly *synthetic* viewers): this only produces the rows the website shows.
>    With a catalogue this small, recall@10 is not meaningful (10 of 17 titles is more than half the catalogue).

Numbers below are generated by `ml/make_results.py` from `ml/artifacts/*.json`. Reproduce with
`make ml-benchmark` (see README).

## 1. MovieLens benchmark

Data: MovieLens from [GroupLens](https://grouplens.org/datasets/movielens/) (F. Maxwell Harper and Joseph A. Konstan,
*The MovieLens Datasets: History and Context*, ACM TiiS 5(4), 2015). Used for research only and **not redistributed**:
`ml/data/` is gitignored. A *positive* is a rating of 4 or more.

**Split:** per-user temporal leave-last-out. Each user's positives are sorted by time; the **last** is the TEST item, the
second-to-last the VALIDATION item, everything earlier is TRAIN, so no future information reaches training.
**Metrics:** full ranking over every item the user has not interacted with (no sampled negatives). recall@10 is the share of
users whose held-out item is in their top 10 (with one held-out item this equals hit rate@10). NDCG@10 rewards ranking it
higher. Ties are counted against the model. Hyper-parameters were chosen on the validation item; the test item was scored
once. **The bar to beat is the popularity baseline** (same list for everyone).

### ml-1m

6,038 users, 3,533 items, 575,281 positives; 6,034 users evaluated; device `mps`; total benchmark time 394 s.

| Model | recall@10 | 95% CI (±) | NDCG@10 | vs popularity | tuned settings |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Popularity (baseline) | **0.0426** | 0.0051 | 0.0205 | - | - |
| ALS (implicit) | **0.0776** | 0.0067 | 0.0397 | 1.82x | factors=64, reg=0.5, alpha=10, iterations=12 |
| Two-tower (PyTorch) | **0.0843** (±0.0016 over 3 seeds) | 0.0070 | 0.0418 | 1.98x | dim=64, lr=0.003, temp=0.1, epochs_max=40 |

### ml-latest-small

609 users, 6,298 items, 48,580 positives; 603 users evaluated; device `mps`; total benchmark time 42 s.

| Model | recall@10 | 95% CI (±) | NDCG@10 | vs popularity | tuned settings |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Popularity (baseline) | **0.0398** | 0.0156 | 0.0208 | - | - |
| ALS (implicit) | **0.0647** | 0.0196 | 0.0322 | 1.62x | factors=64, reg=0.05, alpha=10, iterations=12 |
| Two-tower (PyTorch) | **0.0591** (±0.0028 over 3 seeds) | 0.0188 | 0.0318 | 1.49x | dim=64, lr=0.001, temp=0.1, epochs_max=40 |

**How to read this.** Both learned models (ALS and the two-tower) beat the popularity baseline by roughly 2x on ML-1M. The difference *between* ALS and the two-tower is within the 95% confidence interval, so this benchmark does **not** show that one is better than the other. The production job uses `two_tower` only because it has the higher point estimate on the largest dataset run (a tie-break, not a finding); ALS would serve equally well.

## 2. bhavs production run (live rows on the website)

Last run: model `two_tower`, 199 users, 17 titles, 1118 positive interactions,
746 rows written to the `recommendations` table in 2.0 s.

**Data caveat:** almost all of these users are **synthetic** (generated by `scripts/simulate_users`, clearly labelled
`synthetic-NNNN`); real users are the handful who signed up. The simulator itself has taste clusters, so the models
recovering them shows the pipeline works end to end; it says nothing about real-world quality.

Rows produced per user: **Top picks for you** (model scores, watched titles removed), up to two **Because you watched X**
rows (nearest titles to your recent favourites in the model's item space) and **Popular on bhavs**. Brand-new users get
the GLOBAL rows: overall popularity plus **popularity by genre** (cold start).

Offline sanity check on this synthetic data (135 users, 17 titles; same temporal split; *not a benchmark*). Every number is near the ceiling and popularity is as good as anything, because a top-10 list covers more than half of a 17-title catalogue, so almost any list contains the held-out title. This is exactly why model quality is measured on MovieLens (section 1) and not here:

| Model | recall@10 | NDCG@10 |
|---|---|---|
| Popularity (baseline) | 0.896 | 0.583 |
| ALS (implicit) | 0.822 | 0.383 |
| Two-tower (PyTorch) | 0.881 | 0.538 |
